## Acceptance gate

- Source frame unchanged.
- Target frame has the exact requested dimensions.
- Every locked item is present or has an explicit blocked/approved exception.
- Primary message/task, primary action, and essential media subject remain clear.
- No required text clipping, overlap, unintended overflow, or geometric image stretching.
- Components remain instances and variables/styles remain bound where available.
- Full-frame screenshot and high-risk close-ups reviewed.
